Products

One product live. More being built.

We don't just build software for clients — we build our own. PruneSEO is our first product: a crawl-based technical SEO audit platform for large websites.

PruneSEOA product by Autollmate

Find the URLs Google isn't indexing. Fix the SEO waste.

PruneSEO crawls your site, groups URLs into patterns, and turns thousands of technical findings into a prioritized action plan. No Search Console connection required.

  • Crawl-first inventory with six finding families: http, metadata, canonical, robots, links and patterns
  • Pattern-level analysis that turns tens of thousands of rows into a handful of templates
  • Observed vs inferred labels, severity, confidence and affected URL counts
  • PDF and CSV exports your team can hand straight to a developer
  • Technical SEO
  • Indexation audit
  • Crawl analysis
