FAQ

Evidence first. No guarantees we can't keep.

PruneSEO tells you what is wrong with your indexation and what to change. It does not guarantee that Google indexes anything, and it does not promise rankings.

What is PruneSEO?

PruneSEO is a crawl-based technical SEO audit platform for large websites. It builds a URL inventory from sitemaps and internal links, groups URLs into patterns, and turns findings about indexation, canonicals, robots directives and broken links into a prioritized action plan.

Does PruneSEO replace Google Search Console?

No. PruneSEO is crawl-first and does not require a Search Console connection. It complements Search Console by showing what a crawler can actually reach and by grouping URL-level signals into patterns, rather than relying only on what Google has already indexed.

Does PruneSEO fix SEO issues automatically?

No. PruneSEO describes findings and recommends next steps with supporting evidence. Applying a change is left to you or your developer. It never deploys changes to your website.

Can PruneSEO handle large websites?

Yes. Plans support up to 50,000 unique URLs per full crawl, 25 websites on the Agency plan, and four crawl runs per website per month. Pattern analysis is designed for large inventories.

How much does PruneSEO cost?

Paid plans start at $19 per month for one website and 5,000 URLs per crawl, up to $149 per month for agencies managing 25 websites and 50,000 URLs per crawl. You can start with a public audit before choosing a plan.
