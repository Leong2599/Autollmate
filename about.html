About us

A small studio with a product habit.

Autollmate started in 2017 as two engineers automating operations for a logistics company. Nine years later we are a 38-person agency and product studio — and we still believe the best software is the kind you stop noticing.

Why we exist

Most software projects fail on the edges: unclear scope, manual handoffs, and systems nobody wants to maintain. We build for the edges. That means automation, observability, and code your team can own after we leave.

Why we built a product

Running an agency teaches you where software breaks. PruneSEO came from a recurring technical SEO problem we could not solve with existing tools, so we built the audit workflow we wanted — and made it a product.

How we think

Four principles we don't negotiate.

Evidence over opinion

We measure before we argue. That applies to product decisions, performance work, and the findings our own product reports.

Ship small, learn fast

Working software every two weeks beats a perfect plan six months from now. Every sprint ends with something you can actually use.

Own the outcome

We are not done when the invoice clears. We are done when the metric moved, the team is trained, and the system stays up.

Leave the code better

Documentation, tests, and honest architecture decisions. The next engineer should have an easier time than we did.
