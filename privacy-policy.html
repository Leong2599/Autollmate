Privacy policy

How Autollmate handles personal information submitted through this website. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Who we are

Autollmate is a software development agency and product studio. This website is operated by Autollmate. For privacy questions, contact hello@autollmate.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. When you submit a contact form, we collect the name, email address, company, project details, and budget range you choose to share. When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard request data such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamps for security and reliability.
  • Analytics. If analytics are enabled, we use aggregated usage data to understand which pages are useful. We do not sell personal information.

How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries and provide requested services.
  • To send the newsletter if you have asked to receive it.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases

Where GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests to respond to business enquiries and operate the website, consent for newsletter communications, and contract performance where we provide services. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies necessary for it to function, plus any analytics cookies described at the point they are set. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may then behave differently.

Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate the site and communicate with you — for example, hosting, email, and analytics providers — under appropriate confidentiality and data-processing terms. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship, and newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe. We may retain limited records where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, or to receive a portable copy. To exercise a right, email hello@autollmate.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Children

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@autollmate.com.
